# Demo video script (about 4 minutes)

Use the demo account (Profile, Sign in, "Use the demo account").

1. **What it is (15 s).** Home. "People fund the reach of a message, not the cause." Point at the estimate and the fair-question card.
2. **Discover (30 s).** Explore: search "shade", pick a category, change sort, open Filters. Show no-results by searching nonsense.
3. **Read and trust (40 s).** Open a campaign: story, sources, "How is your support spent?", ad preview, review (labelled demo), updates. Save it.
4. **Back it (50 s).** Back this campaign, pick $25, show reach and fees, tick the acknowledgement, demo checkout. Show Declined once, then Approved. Show the receipt and updated progress.
5. **Create (80 s).** Create tab, Create manually. Use "Draft it for me", fix the title, add a source, generate a cover, choose a platform, set the goal on the last step, accept terms, Submit. Show the timeline.
6. **Track (40 s).** Profile, My campaigns: status tabs. Open the new campaign, press the demo reviewer buttons to approve and open for backing. Open one Promoting and one Completed campaign to show the report with measured vs estimated numbers.
7. **Activity and impact (20 s).** Activity tab with unread badge, then My impact.
8. **Close (10 s).** One line on the stack: Expo, Express, Postgres, server-enforced lifecycle.
